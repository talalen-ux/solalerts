Form accumulators
Model slips · any market
Prices are fair odds, the break-even price. Back a slip only when your bookmaker pays more; margin compounds across legs.
Wildcards · long shots
Each wildcard takes the most likely result from 10, 15 or 19 different matches. Every leg is around 90%, yet one miss sinks the slip. At a typical 5% bookmaker margin per leg you get back about 61p, 48p and 40p per £1 staked on average. Treat them as a small-stake bit of fun, not a plan.
How the numbers are made
Team strength
Attack and defence ratings per league from three seasons of ESPN results, older matches fading. Refit every week with the latest scores.

Injuries & bans
Key forward out −6% goals; midfielder −3%/+2% conceded; defender +4%; keeper +6%. Doubts count half, capped at 15%.
