How the numbers are made

Team strength Applied

Attack and defence ratings from three seasons per league, older matches fading. Results after the public feed stopped (20 Sept 2026) come from a researched ledger that grows each week.

Last 5 games Filter only

Picks the form-winners list. A separate form term added nothing to predictions on 2023-26 data, so it never raises a probability.

Travel distance Tested · no gain

Shown on each fixture. Its effect flipped sign between seasons and never improved held-out predictions.

Injuries & bans Where verified

Key forward out −6% goals; midfielder −3% / +2% conceded; defender +4%; keeper +6%. Doubts count half, capped at 15%.

Europe midweek Applied

A Thursday-to-Sunday turnaround costs −3% goals and +2% conceded, when the midweek game is known.

Markets Applied

Results, double chance, totals, both-to-score, team 2+ goals, win-to-nil. "Team scores 1+" legs are left out: books load margin onto them.
