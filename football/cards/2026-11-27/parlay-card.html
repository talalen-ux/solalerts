Matchday Parlay Card

fixtures from the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1, priced by a Dixon-Coles goals model refit with this season's results and adjusted for team news where it could be verified. Each slip shows its fair odds: the break-even price. Back a slip only if your bookmaker pays more.

Form winners

Teams that won at least 3 of their last 5 league games and are favourites again, ranked by the model's chance they win. Over 2023-26, in-form favourites won as often as the model said or slightly more (strong favourites: 73% predicted, 77% won), while big favourites out of form fell short (71% predicted, 66% won). Form is used to pick teams, not to raise their odds.

The slips

What went into the numbers

Team strength Applied

Attack and defence ratings from three seasons per league, with older matches fading (half weight after about 15 months). Results after the public feed stopped (20 Sept 2026) come from a researched ledger that grows each week.

Last 5 games Tested · no gain

Shown on every fixture. A separate form term was fitted on 2023-26 (5,235 matches) and scored on held-out seasons: it changed log loss by +0.0000 to +0.0008, i.e. no better. The decayed ratings already carry recent form.

Travel distance Tested · no gain

Stadium-to-stadium distance is on every fixture. Its fitted effect flipped sign from season to season (−0.012 to +0.007 per log-km) and never improved held-out predictions, so home advantage stays one number per league.

Injuries & bans Where verified

Key forward out: −6% to his team's goals. Key midfielder −3% / +2% conceded. Key defender +4% conceded, keeper +6%. Doubts count half, squad players a quarter, capped at 15%. Applied only to teams with injury news found this week.

Europe midweek Applied

A Thursday-to-Sunday turnaround costs −3% goals and +2% conceded. Applied to teams whose midweek European match is known.

Market choice Applied

Legs come from results, double chance, totals, both-to-score, team 2+ goals and win-to-nil. The backtest shows the edge is in result markets; legs such as "team scores at least once" are excluded because books load the most margin onto them.

Read before you bet

  • Team news is applied only where it could be verified (see each fixture's notes). Elsewhere matches are priced on results and ratings; check confirmed line-ups before kick-off.
  • Recent results come from research, not an official feed. Some rest on one source. Unfilled gaps this season: .
  • Fair odds are not value. The model is calibrated (in backtests a 75% leg won 74.6% of the time), so a slip at its fair price breaks even. Margin of 5-10% per leg compounds over a parlay. Take a slip only above its fair odds.
  • Legs on different matches are treated as independent. Same-game legs are priced jointly from the scoreline grid.
  • Stake small. A 5-fold at fair 7.0 loses six times in seven on average.
